The original, finessed

Clear brief this time: the mark was never the problem. Your double stroke stays exactly as it is — two ensō strokes, Moss leading, Sage following, closed into a ring. What changes is the finish: the strokes shed weight, the seam between them becomes deliberate, and the type stops shouting.

Below: the same mark at three stroke weights, then three settings of the wordmark — all thinner, smaller, quieter. My recommendation sits on top.
