# Outlier Medicine — Brand Identity

The mark: an open circle (ensō drawn as the boolean of two circles) with a single
point standing off the circumference — the outlier. Reads as an O monogram.

## Files

- `brand-presentation.html` — full identity presentation: rationale, construction,
  lockups, color, typography, and application mockups. Open in any browser.
- `logo-mark.svg` — primary mark, Moss `#575C2E` on transparent.
- `logo-mark-reversed.svg` — Bone `#EFECE0` on Deep Field `#2C2E1D`.

## Geometry

- Outer circle: center (120, 120), r 100
- Inner circle: center (130, 110), r 88
- Aperture: 59.5°, opening northeast
- Point: r 10.5, on the 45° axis at radius 113 from center
